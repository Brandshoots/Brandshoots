## How it works (6 steps, icon row)
1. **Content Planning** – Monthly ideas, content direction and brand positioning.
2. **Scripts** – Hooks, scripts and structure for every reel.
3. **Production** – Shoot planning and execution as per your plan.
4. **Post-Production** – Editing, captions, pacing and final delivery.
5. **Social Media Management** – Instagram, Facebook and YouTube publishing and scheduling.
6. **Performance** – A monthly report with insights for the next month.
